Welcome to Wild Rescues. Our mission is to rescue, rehabilitate, and release wildlife back to their natural enviroment. We are a non profit oganization dedicated to helping injured wildlife.

Our staff provides around-the-clock care for wildlife in need. We also provide long-term care for wildlife that cannot be released back into their natural enviroment.

Will you partner with us? We need caring volunteers to help care for our animals. You can also donate, become a member, or become a sponsor. Contact us today.
