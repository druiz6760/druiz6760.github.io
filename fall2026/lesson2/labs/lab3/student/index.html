Did you know your school has clubs?

Yes, your school has many club for you to join. You may think that there is no time or profit for in being part of a group. But that is far from the truth. You have a lot to gain from club activities. Our goal is to let you know what are the good reasons to join a club and to let you know there is a club for everyone!
