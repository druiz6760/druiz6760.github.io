Flavor

Chef Burton mentions that cookbooks don't teach intuition and instinct. To get those, you need to know technique, flavor, and execution. Flavor is important. I would say flavor is the sum of the properties of spices on the human palate that come from seasonings and combining them in the right quantities. Chef Burton teaches that it comes from knowing the five basic flavor structures: salty, bitter, sweet, umami, and sour. When you learn and know how to harmonize these, you can create and adjust your dishes on the fly, but it takes practice.

Did you know?

The golden ratio to get the right salty flavor in your dishes is this: take the weight of your unnsalted dish and add 0.5% -1% of salt to it and you're there. The perfect seasinging threshhold.
