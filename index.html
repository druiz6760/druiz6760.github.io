    About

    I'm Diana Ruiz. I am currently pursuing a website development degree at Gwinnett Technical College. I chose this discipline because coding is engaging and fun. I see it as an artistic expression and a process that involves organization, planning, and style. It seems like a big endeavor, but I think coding can refine other areas of my life. By the end of this class, I hope to be able to create simple websites and have good foundational knowledge. Outside of school, I enjoy spending time outdoors, watching movies, and cooking.

    My Webpage

    For my first webpage, I decided to center the theme on cooking. I've been cooking for a while and recently began choosing receipes from two or three YouTube chefs. I figure that I would choose a handful of receipes to master and give a little attention to the science and cooking techniques used. Why? I think it will help me level up my skills in the kitchen. My webpage will be my digital food journal.
