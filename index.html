About Me

Hi, I’m Diana Ruiz.

I’m a student at Gwinnett Technical College who is studying website development. I enjoy coding because it allows people to connect and bring their ideas to the digital landscape. Creating projects solo or in a group is great fun, and seeing my skills and understanding grow brings a sense of fulfillment.

This semester, I’m studying HTML, CSS, and JavaScript by creating projects that are useful, accessible, and fun to use. By the end of this semester, I hope to possess the skills that enable the making of simple and responsive webpages for small businesses in my local community. My long-term goal is to become a front-end web developer. In the short-term, I would love to work as a freelancer while I pursue my studies.

In my spare time, I enjoy cooking, working out, and being outdoors.
