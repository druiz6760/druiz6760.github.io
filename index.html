ABOUT ME

I'm Diana Ruiz. I am currently pursuing a website development degree at Gwinnett Technical College. I want to learn to make websites because I enjoy coding, become happy when my code works and I comprehend what's happening under the hood. My hobbies include the outdoors and playing guitar. I hope that by the end of this semester I can code simple websites. Thanks for stopping by.

ABOUT MY PAGE

For my first webpage, I decided to center the theme on cooking. I've been cooking for a while and recently began choosing receipes from two or three YouTube chefs. I figure that I would choose a handful of receipes to master and give a little attention to the science and cooking techniques used. Why? I think it will help me level up my skills in the kitchen. My webpage will be my digital food journal.
