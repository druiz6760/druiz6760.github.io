About Diana's Kitchen

Welcome!

I've been learning to cook for years, mostly by following recipes, but that hasn't given me the instinct to put a dish together from scratch. I've decided to experiment with a different approach. I'll study a little about cooking techniques, cooking tools, and publish what I learn alongside a small selection of dishes I make. Each with a photo and a note on what I picked up along the way. Learning to cook is a tough skill to master, but I believe that practice and knowledge can get anyone there. Let the journey begin!

Meet The Chef

I'm Diana Ruiz. I am currently pursuing a website development degree at Gwinnett Technical College. I want to learn to make websites because I enjoy coding and become happy when both my code works and I comprehend what's happening under the hood. My hobbies include the outdoors and playing guitar. I hope that by the end of this semester I can code simple websites. Thanks for stopping by.
