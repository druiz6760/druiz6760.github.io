About Diana's Kitchen

I like to eat delicious food. Eating out can save time and mildly promise a tasty dish; however, there is no guarantee. I decided, with suggestions from family and friends, to try my hand at cooking my own meals. It takes time, planning, and discipline. Yet, I am in control of the quality of ingredients and portions. Overall, it has been worth it.

Cooking meals from scratch has taught me to care about quality ingredients, animal welfare, and sustainable farming practices. A meal is so much more. Food can bring people together, allow you to express your creativity and knowledge, and be a gift crafted with care.

I decided to center the theme on cooking from scratch for my first webpage because I want to express my passion for cooking using code.
